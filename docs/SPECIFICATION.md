# Implementation specification

## System

One Linux process owns a world; it advances at a fixed logical timestep independently of wall time and connected browsers. A single simulation lock serializes commands, ticks and saves. Background process workers perform pure neural inference; the parent owns learning, mutation and action RNG. Threaded HTTP serves read snapshots and validated commands. This simple initial transport should be replaced or bounded behind a production proxy for larger deployments.

The initial world is a 96×64 periodic grid with elevation, moisture, fertility, grass, trees, ore, fire, temperature, runoff, suspended sediment and lava fields. Periodic multiscale value noise and geography-specific masks generate initial terrain. Eight geography presets and eleven climate profiles plus a natural mosaic are selectable. World sizes are 64×48, 96×64 and 144×96. Staggered local runoff transfers water and suspended sediment downhill; lava spreads downhill and cools into rock. Periodic lowest-spill catchments also generate visible river channels, but there is no calibrated watershed water budget or explicit lake storage. The biome renderer derives colors and tiny tree sprites from state/coordinates. There are no downloaded assets. Noise is periodic, but shaped geography masks and settlement distances are not uniformly periodic: seams remain a known prototype limitation.

## Organisms

Each organism has ID, species, position, energy, age, generation, size, fertility, thermal preference, parent IDs, culture, materials, reward baseline, eight recurrent memory values, 280 action-policy weights and nine value-head weights. Observations: bias, energy, age, local grass/trees/moisture/fire, season and four directional food/prey indicators, four predator/fire indicators and four human material indicators, and eight previous hidden-state values. Rays extend three tiles with inverse-distance attenuation; water and high mountains occlude them. Living prey/threat counts are indexed once before each tick’s decisions. Network: 28 inputs, 8 tanh hidden units, 7 softmax actions. Actions: four cardinal movements, eat, reproduce, species-dependent work/rest. The action set and network topology are fixed in this version; previous hidden activations make inference recurrent. Predators sense living grazers rather than grass. Actions are still sampled from learned policies; sensing does not force hunting or avoidance.

Training: immediate energy delta and reproduction/construction bonuses feed a bounded advantage against an exponential moving baseline. REINFORCE updates both policy layers. A separate bounded value head learns one-step temporal-difference error from the following state and applies a small, thresholded future-value correction to the preceding neural action. A successful predator hunt also credits the preceding move, if any. Both policy and value weights are clipped to [-4,4]. Children can have two nearby compatible parents; their weights recombine before mutation. Size, fertility and temperature preference are inherited with bounded mutation. Births enter a bounded ancestry record, and coarse thermal/size ecotypes are counted. Inheritance currently includes learned weights (Lamarckian design choice); document future alternatives. A frozen-policy mode supports paired experiments and is persisted in saves. Every 20 ticks, humans sharing a settlement and culture can weakly imitate the policy and value head of a peer with a higher recent reward estimate. The mentor must have at least ten policy updates; the bounded blend is 2% and clears stale action credit. This local mechanism has no validated social benefit. Backpropagation through time, validated long-horizon behavioral gains and topology evolution remain outstanding.

## Ecology and humans

Grass and trees occupy explicit local cohorts with bounded population counts and inherited temperature/moisture trait means. Seed dispersal carries those traits with small founding variation; climate fitness and competition affect germination and biomass. Flooding clears cohorts, and fire reduces them. Feeding drains biomass and fertility, death returns nutrients, fire consumes vegetation and enriches soil, and erosion changes land availability. Humans gather timber and minerals, found settlements after enough wood, and take farming, woodcutting, mining or building jobs according to local shortages. Houses support up to four-member households; household food stores feed members. Repeated travel creates roads that reduce movement cost. Culture labels transmit through inheritance and local influence. Surplus food can be bartered for scarce ore or wood through passable-land caravans; cargo moves after route-dependent travel and paths gain traffic. Empty towns lose food and houses before abandonment. Institutions, diplomacy and technology remain future work.

## Persistence and API

Schema version 18 JSON stores full precision state, both sets of neural weights, pending temporal credit and Python RNG state. Write-to-temporary + fsync + atomic rename protects the last completed save from partial writes. Only trusted server-owned saves are loaded. `/api/state` is a rendering snapshot and is not a save. `/api/command` accepts preview, new_world, pause, speed, save and tool. `/health` reports simulation failure. Browser remote access uses administrator and optional read-only spectator bearer tokens entered by the user; tokens remain in JS memory and are not put in URLs/storage. Administrator-only metrics expose tick time, population, training steps and active connections. The server caps active connections and per-peer API requests. Commands reject cross-origin Origin headers and non-JSON bodies. Remote bind requires a 24+ character token. Terminate TLS at a reverse proxy; do not expose this development HTTP server directly to the internet.

## Scaling

0 workers selects process affinity CPUs; inference parallelizes when batches justify overhead. Tiny worlds run scalar inference. All rule application and gradient training currently remain serial, so more workers do not guarantee faster ticks. Optional PyTorch CUDA inference batches distinct policies with bmm; CPU/GPU floating-point differences can change sampled histories. A compute-enabled vGPU must expose CUDA; virtual display adapters cannot substitute. GPU training/vectorized state are roadmap items. The browser only renders and sends commands.

## Save migration

Version 1 policies are migrated by retaining each hidden unit’s original 12 input connections, adding eight zero-weight sensory connections, and retaining all output connections. RNG state is preserved. Perception semantics changed, so migrated worlds do not reproduce version 1 trajectories; deterministic continuation applies within version 2. Version 3 adds tile temperature and world geography/biome metadata; old saves get temperate temperature defaults without changing their existing terrain. Version 4 adds grass and tree seed banks initialized from existing vegetation in older saves. Version 5 adds learning mode, hunt metrics and one-step movement credit state; older saves default to learning enabled. Version 6 adds runoff, suspended sediment, lava and plant cohort state; missing fields derive from existing vegetation and climate. Version 7 adds parent IDs, thermal preference, sexual birth counts and bounded ancestry records. Version 8 adds household membership and food, occupations, road/traffic fields and stable town IDs. Version 9 adds bounded in-transit barter shipments and town abandonment state. Version 10 expands policies from 20 to 28 inputs with zero initial memory connections in migrated policies and saves per-organism hidden state. Pending move traces are expanded too. Version 11 adds mineral nutrient and organic litter pools to tiles; older saves initialize them from fertility and vegetation. Nutrients constrain plant growth, litter decomposes, and runoff can transport dissolved minerals. Version 12 adds a bounded inherited mate-recognition signal and counts candidate encounters/rejections; older organisms receive a neutral signal and zero historical counts. Adults require signal and thermal compatibility for two-parent births, and incompatible co-located adults prevent the solitary-birth fallback for that attempt. Signal bins and encounter rejection rates measure reproductive divergence but are not validated biological species. Version 13 persists the coarse cloud grid. Older saves initialize clouds from a separate seed stream, leaving organism RNG untouched. Simultaneous periodic advection and terrain-sensitive precipitation alter moisture, runoff and fire; this is not a physical atmospheric solver. Version 14 adds bounded river-channel strength per tile. Every 32 ticks a lowest-spill ocean drainage tree accumulates upstream wetness, refreshes visible channels, and slowly carves sediment. Older saves initialize channels to zero. This approximation does not store lakes or conserve an explicit watershed water budget. Version 15 persists human migration destinations and bounded land routes; older organisms default to no plan. Version 16 persists a bounded burn-scar field; older tiles default to no scar. Fire and volcanic damage briefly favors grass regrowth over trees, and scarred biome labels fade as vegetation returns. Version 17 adds inherited value-head weights, pending temporal credit and critic update counts. Older organisms start with neutral value heads and no pending credit. Version 18 persists each organism’s recent-reward estimate and lesson count, plus the world lesson total; older saves default to zero. Save writes upgrade to version 18; keep a backup if rollback is needed.

## New worlds and powers

Default startup has no active world and does not read the previous save. Authenticated clients fetch setup options from /api/state, request a preview with server-generated random seed, and create the matching landscape. The server retains at most 16 preview configurations and verifies selected seed/options and world epoch before replacement. A stale client cannot replace a newer world. Existing live or on-disk saves are archived before replacement; generation failure leaves the live world intact. New worlds are shared: page refreshes reconnect, not reset. Explicit --load opts into resume.

Powers are defined in powers.py with a shared server/UI catalog. They report tile changes, spawned/altered/killed organisms and destroyed settlements. No-effect casts are explicit. Water clears trees/grass/fire immediately. Life casts choose habitable tiles in the brush and honor the population cap. Temperature affects growth and metabolic cost; biome brushes change climate/soil/vegetation rather than only color. Renderer biome classification responds to climate, vegetation cover and recent burning. Grassland, woodland and burn-scar presets and brushes are available. Rain creates moving surface runoff, volcanoes emit moving lava, and periodic lowest-spill catchments refresh river channels; none of these form a calibrated fluid or watershed budget. Cognitive decisions remain the recurrent online-trained 28→8→7 networks; the mutation power changes both neural components, size and thermal preference, but does not evolve topology.
